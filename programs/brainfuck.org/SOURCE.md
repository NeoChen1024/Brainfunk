# brainfuck.org

Programs by Daniel B. Cristofani, downloaded byte for byte from <https://brainfuck.org/> on
2026-11-08. `programs.toml` lists the URL of each file.

## License

The index page of the site says, verbatim:

> (Note: I'm licensing all of these under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
> International License. Haven't got around to putting that into all the individual files.)

and links to <http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>. Several files also carry the
notice themselves. `LICENSE` is the license's legal code, from
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.txt>.

The files here are unmodified. Anything derived from them, such as inputs written for them,
lives under `inputs/` and is offered under the same license.

## Not included

The site also has pages, C sources (`dbc.c`, `qdb.c`, `sbi.c`, `bcci.c`), images and text
notes. Only the `.b` programs are kept here.
