# Unattributed programs

**The author and the license of these programs are unknown.** They are kept, unmodified,
because they are widely copied and used, and the corpus should not depend on one copy
staying online. If you know who wrote one of them, or under what terms, please say so, and it
will be credited or removed as the author wishes.
